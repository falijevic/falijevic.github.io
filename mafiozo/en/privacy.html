Privacy Policy

Mafiozo does not collect your data

Last updated: November 26, 2026

In short: the game does not collect, send or sell any personal data. No sign-up, ads, analytics or tracking.

What stays on your phone

To make the game easier to play, the following is stored on the device itself (never on a server):

  • player names and the seating order,
  • player photos, if you added them (one small picture per player),
  • the role setup, rules and group experience you chose,
  • the sound and language settings,
  • a game in progress, so you can resume it.

This data stays on your device. Deleting the app deletes it too.

Camera and photo library

You can take a player photo with the camera or pick one from your library. The camera asks for your permission and is used only at that moment. With the library, the game sees only the photo you pick, not the rest of your library. The photo is scaled down, stored inside the game on your phone and never sent anywhere. Removing a player removes their photo.

Network requests

At launch the game asks Apple's public iTunes Lookup service whether a newer version is on the App Store. The request carries nothing about you, only the app's identifier and the App Store country.

Purchases

If the game ever offers extra content, Apple handles the purchase entirely. We never see or store payment details.

Children

The game collects no data from anyone, children included.
