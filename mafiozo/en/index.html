Mafiozo

The town sleeps. The family doesn't. A party game for 5 to 15 players on one phone.

How to play

  1. Enter the names and deal the roles. The phone goes around the table and everyone sees their card in secret.

  2. At night everyone makes a move, and every move looks the same. Nobody across the table knows who picked what.

  3. In the morning the game announces what happened. Then the town argues: who is lying?

  4. A secret vote decides who the town throws out. The town wins when the mafia is gone; the mafia wins when it matches the town in numbers.

  • MafiaPicks a victim every night. Plays innocent by day.
  • LadyWorks for the mafia and silences one player for the next day.
  • PoliceChecks one player every night.
  • DoctorProtects one player from the mafia every night.
  • CitizensNo power, but a vote and an instinct.

FAQ

Do I need internet?
No. The game plays completely offline.
How many players?
From 5 to 15, on one phone.
Is the setup fair?
The recommended setup is the one closest to 50/50 for your player count, checked in thousands of simulated games.
Someone has to leave mid-game?
Tap × at the top right, then “Someone has to leave: remove a player”. Their role stays secret until the game ends.
Left the app?
The game is saved. Tap “Resume game” on the home screen.
How do I change the language or turn off sound?
The globe and the speaker are on the home screen. The game also respects the phone's silent mode.
